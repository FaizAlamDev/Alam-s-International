About us

Our products speak
for themselves.

Alam’s International specialises in leather tanning, leather processing and the manufacturing of finished leather products for domestic and international markets. Based in Kanpur — a city with a rich heritage in the leather industry — we bring together craftsmanship, manufacturing expertise and a commitment to quality to meet our clients’ specific requirements.

Our capabilities cover the entire production process, from raw hides to fully finished leather and ready-to-use leather goods. With expertise in tanning and finishing, we offer leather belts, bags, wallets, safety shoes and finished leather hides. Managing multiple stages of production under one roof gives buyers greater flexibility, consistent quality and solutions tailored to different business needs.

Every client has unique expectations. Whether you require leather in a specific finish, texture, colour or thickness, or finished products developed to your designs and specifications, we work to bring your requirements to life — focusing on precision, durability and attention to detail at every stage.

  • Own tannery & workshop
  • Leather finished in-house
  • Made to your colour & design
  • Export-focused production

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Everything you need to know before you send an enquiry. If your question isn’t here, write to us — we’re happy to help.

Still have a question?
Can you produce to my own design or sample?

Yes. Send a sketch, a reference photo or a physical sample. We will develop the leather, hardware and finish, and confirm the details with you before production.

What is your minimum order quantity?

MOQ depends on the product, material and finish. Tell us what you have in mind and we will confirm the smallest viable run for your order.

Can you tan leather in a specific colour or finish?

Yes. Colour, grain, hand-feel and finish can be developed to your shade or sample. Share a swatch or reference and we will match it as closely as the leather allows.

Do you supply samples before a bulk order?

We can arrange development samples so you can check the leather and workmanship. Sample cost and timing are confirmed with your enquiry.

Which countries do you export to?

We work with buyers in India and overseas. Share your destination and we will confirm shipping and documentation for your market.

What are your lead times and payment terms?

Both depend on the product, quantity and season. We confirm lead time and payment terms in writing at the quotation stage, before production begins.

How do I place an order?

Send an enquiry with the product code, quantity and any customisation. We will reply with a quotation, sample options and next steps.
